Before you play

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You inspect evidence and make decisions through the game’s workstation interfaces. An in-game tutorial introduces your tasks.

How do I move around and interact?

Use W A S D to move and the mouse to look around. Press E to interact, click to use the task panels, and use Esc for pause or back actions. Follow the prompts for each scene.

Can I play on Mac, mobile, or in a browser?

The available download is for Windows PCs. The game runs on your computer.
